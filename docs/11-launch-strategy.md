# 11 — Launch Strategy: 6-Month Plan

## Pre-Launch (Month 0 — Before Beta)

### Team & Infrastructure
- Core team: 2 backend engineers, 1 mobile engineer, 1 ML engineer, 1 designer, 1 PM, 1 growth marketer
- Infrastructure: GCP project setup, Stripe Connect onboarding, domain + SSL
- Legal: Terms of service, privacy policy, GDPR/CCPA compliance review
- App stores: iOS App Store + Google Play developer accounts

### Beta Cohort Recruitment
Target: **500 beta users** selected for quality, not quantity

**Selection criteria**:
- Mix of sellers (60%) and buyers (40%)
- Diverse demographics: parents, students, retirees, workers
- At least 100 users who have never used a resale platform before (our core non-tech audience)
- Geographic clustering: 3 metro areas (Atlanta, Denver, Phoenix) for local marketplace density

**Recruitment channels**:
- Personal networks (founders + team)
- Local community Facebook groups (parenting groups, neighborhood groups)
- Reddit (r/personalfinance, r/malefrugalwear, r/budgetfashion)
- Waiting list page with referral mechanism

---

## Month 1: Closed Beta

### Goal: Learn, not grow

**Focus**:
- Do users understand the agent concept? (User interviews, 50+)
- Is listing generation accurate enough? (Quality audit of first 1,000 listings)
- Does the negotiation feel right? (Interview sellers about their comfort level)
- Where do users get confused? (Session recording via FullStory)

**Success Metrics**:
- 80% of new listings created without user editing the description
- 70% of negotiations completed without user intervention
- 60% of beta users complete at least 1 transaction
- NPS ≥ 50 from beta users

**Infrastructure Milestones**:
- Core listing pipeline operational
- A2ANP negotiation protocol v1 live
- Stripe payment + escrow working end-to-end
- Basic fraud rules deployed

**Team Operations**:
- Daily standups with weekly beta user office hours
- Fraud moderation team on call 9AM–9PM
- Personal support: every beta user has a direct Slack channel with the team

---

## Month 2: Beta Expansion + Category Focus

### Goal: Nail one category before expanding

**Launch Category: Kid's Items & Baby Gear**

Why this category first:
1. **High seller motivation**: Parents have tons of items, want to clear space, don't know what it's worth
2. **Clear buyer need**: Parents of younger kids actively look for cheaper gear
3. **High item density**: Strollers, car seats, clothes, toys — many items per household
4. **Word-of-mouth natural**: Parent networks are tight; one satisfied parent tells 20 others
5. **Safety is paramount**: Our trust features resonate immediately with parents

**Month 2 Goals**:
- 2,500 beta users (invite-only expansion from Month 1 users + new waitlist)
- 5,000 listings in Kid's category
- 1,000 completed transactions
- Cover Atlanta, Denver, Phoenix fully; add one more city (Dallas)

**Content Strategy**:
- Blog: "How to clear your kid's closet in a weekend"
- SEO: Target "sell baby stuff near me", "used stroller price", "how much is a Uppababy worth"
- Partner with 5 parenting bloggers/influencers (micro, authentic, not mega)

**Referral Mechanic Launch**:
- "Invite a friend to sell → both get 0% fees on your next transaction"
- Referred users tagged in system → track referral network growth

---

## Month 3: Public Beta + Second Category

### Goal: Open the doors, maintain quality

**Open Beta Launch**:
- Remove invite gate
- App Store + Google Play listing live
- Basic PR: local news in launch cities ("App does your selling for you")

**Second Category: Consumer Electronics**
- Phones, laptops, tablets, game consoles
- Higher transaction values → more revenue per deal
- Tech-adjacent users who understand value of resale

**Month 3 Goals**:
- 15,000 registered users
- 25,000 active listings
- $500,000 in GMV
- Average listing-to-sale time: < 10 days

**Growth Mechanics**:
- Viral listing share: Every sold listing generates a shareable card ("I sold my old iPhone for $240 in 3 days, no negotiating")
- Agent achievement notifications: "Your agent saved you 2 hours this week"

---

## Month 4: Community Building + Seller Momentum

### Goal: Build seller supply at scale

**Seller Acquisition Push**:
- "Garage sale alternative" campaign: Target people searching "how to have a garage sale"
- Partner with storage unit companies (their customers are desperate to sell stuff)
- Partner with moving companies ("Moving? Sell what you're not taking")

**Community Features**:
- AgentBay community forum (web): Tips, success stories, Q&A
- Weekly "Big Wins" newsletter: Real transactions, real savings
- Ambassador program: Top sellers get "Trusted Seller" badge, featured on homepage

**Month 4 Goals**:
- 50,000 registered users
- 100,000 active listings
- $2M in GMV
- 500 "power sellers" (>10 transactions each)

**Seller Onboarding Improvement**:
- "First listing guarantee": Agent guarantees first listing goes live in < 3 minutes or user gets 0% fees
- Onboarding checklist shown on first login

---

## Month 5: Monetization Activation + Third Category

### Goal: Turn on revenue sustainably

**Fee Structure Activation**:
- Months 1–3: 0% fees (build trust and GMV)
- Month 4: Announce future fees with 60-day notice
- Month 5: 8% fee activates for new transactions
- Grandfathered: existing power sellers get 6% for 6 months

**Third Category: Furniture & Home**
- High ticket items → high revenue per transaction
- Local-only focus → builds local network density
- Target: people moving, divorcing, downsizing (motivated sellers)

**Plus Subscription Launch**:
- $7.99/month
- Key hook: "Standing searches" — agent monitors for your wishlist 24/7
- Target: buyers who've already gotten a great deal → natural upsell

**Month 5 Goals**:
- 150,000 registered users
- $5M in GMV
- $400K in revenue (fees + subscriptions)
- 5% Plus subscriber conversion

---

## Month 6: Scale & Viral Loop Activation

### Goal: Achieve self-sustaining growth

**Viral Loop Design**:
```
User sells item → Gets more money than expected → Tells friends
                                                        │
User buys item → Gets better deal than expected → Tells friends
                                                        │
Both: Share "AgentBay deal" on social → New signups → 
      New listings → Better inventory → More buyers → ...
```

**Viral Mechanics**:
1. **Deal card sharing**: Auto-generated card with item photo, price achieved, savings vs. retail
2. **"Sold in X hours"** timestamp — creates social proof and FOMO
3. **Referral program V2**: Refer 3 buyers → get Plus free for 1 month
4. **Employer benefit program**: Pitch AgentBay Plus to HR as an employee perk (resale savings)

**Month 6 Goals**:
- 300,000 registered users
- $12M in GMV
- $960,000 in revenue
- K-factor (viral coefficient): > 0.4 (40% of new users come from referrals)
- NPS: > 65
